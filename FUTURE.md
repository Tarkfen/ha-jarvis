# Future Improvements

## Smarter HA Control

- **Scene suggestions** — "I'm watching a movie" triggers a sequence: dim lights, close blinds, set temperature
- **Routine creation** — Jarvis writes HA automations directly from natural language ("every weekday at 7am, turn on the kitchen lights")
- **Anomaly alerts** — proactive notifications ("the front door has been open for 20 minutes")

## Contextual Awareness

- **Presence awareness** — Jarvis knows who's home via HA device trackers and adapts ("the kids are asleep, I'll dim instead of turning off")
- **Room context** — "turn off the lights" defaults to the most likely room based on time of day and presence

## Conversation Upgrades

- **Persistent memory** — remember user preferences across sessions (Celsius vs Fahrenheit, room nicknames like "bureau" = office, "salon" = living room)
- **Multilingual by intent** — auto-detect language per message (French/English already supported in voice)

## Voice Upgrades

- **Physical button trigger** — a Zigbee button press via HA triggers the mic, making it a real hands-free assistant
- **Real TTS voice** — replace browser TTS with ElevenLabs or OpenAI TTS for better quality and mobile support

## Dashboard / History

- **Action log** — visible history of what Jarvis did ("turned off 4 lights, adjusted thermostat to 20°")
- **Energy & sensor summaries** — "how much electricity did we use this week?"
